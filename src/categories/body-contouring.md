Body contouring is about shape rather than weight. Pregnancy, major weight loss, age and genetics can all leave behind stubborn pockets of fat, loose skin or stretched muscles that no diet or exercise routine will fully correct. Liposuction and body contouring sit at the heart of the work of Dr. Junaid Fateh, an FCPS-qualified plastic surgeon practising in Shadman, Lahore, and in Layyah.

The right procedure depends on what is causing the problem. Fat sitting under skin that still has good elasticity often responds well to liposuction alone. Skin that has been stretched and hangs in folds usually needs to be removed, and muscles separated by pregnancy may need repair. Many people have a mix of these, so Dr. Junaid begins with a careful examination and an honest conversation about what each option can realistically achieve for you.

His approach centres on natural-looking results and safety. Rather than chasing dramatic change, he aims for proportions that suit your frame, with treated areas blended smoothly into their surroundings. Every plan includes a proper pre-operative assessment, sensible limits on how much is done in one session and, where needed, staged surgery so your body has time to heal. Like any surgery, body contouring carries risks, and Dr. Junaid goes through them with you openly before you decide.

Planning your recovery matters as much as the operation itself. Most procedures involve compression garments, some time away from work and a period without heavy lifting, so it helps to arrange support from family at home. Planning surgery well ahead of a family wedding or Eid gives swelling time to settle before the day. Recovery times and results vary between people, and you will receive clear guidance at every stage.

Getting started is simple. You can book a consultation through WhatsApp, and if you live abroad or in another city, a video consultation is a convenient first step. Dr. Junaid can review your photos, discuss your goals and outline a likely plan, with the final details confirmed when he examines you in person.

Breast and chest surgery involves some of the most personal decisions a patient can make. For women, it may mean easing the weight of very large breasts, restoring shape after children, adding fullness or rebuilding a breast after cancer treatment. For men, it most often means correcting gynecomastia, the enlarged breast tissue that can leave the chest looking soft or puffy no matter how much time is spent in the gym.

Dr. Junaid Fateh holds an FCPS in Plastic Surgery and practises as a plastic, cosmetic and reconstructive surgeon in Lahore and Layyah. Gynecomastia is one of his signature specialties, and he also performs the wider range of cosmetic and reconstructive breast surgery. Whatever the procedure, his aim stays the same: a result that suits your body, looks natural with or without clothes and is reached as safely as possible.

Every plan begins with a private consultation. Dr. Junaid listens to what is troubling you, examines you carefully and explains which options are realistic, including the times when surgery may not be the right answer. He is clear about scars, recovery and risks, so you can make your decision with full information and without pressure. Bringing a relative along is always fine.

Discretion matters in this area of surgery. Many men feel embarrassed to talk about their chest, and many women prefer to keep their plans within the family. You can reach the clinic first through WhatsApp, send photos privately and, if you live abroad or in another city, begin with a video consultation before you travel.

Recovery differs a great deal between procedures, from a few days after a small nipple correction to several weeks after breast reconstruction. Dr. Junaid and his team guide you through each stage, from preparing for surgery to your follow-up visits, so you are never unsure about the next step or who to contact with a question.

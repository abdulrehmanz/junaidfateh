Your face is the first thing people notice and the part of yourself you see most often. When one feature feels out of balance, or when age starts to show around the eyes, jaw or neck, it can affect how you feel in photos, at work and at family occasions. Facial surgery with Dr. Junaid Fateh is about careful, well-judged changes that help you look like a refreshed version of yourself.

Dr. Junaid is a plastic, cosmetic and reconstructive surgeon who practises in Lahore. He holds an FCPS in Plastic Surgery from the College of Physicians & Surgeons Pakistan, trained at Jinnah Burn & Reconstructive Surgery Centre in Lahore, and is a member of the Pakistan Association of Plastic Surgeons. His reconstructive training shapes how he approaches cosmetic work, with close attention to how tissue heals and where scars will sit.

Good facial surgery starts with diagnosis rather than a fixed menu. Dr. Junaid looks at how your features relate to one another, because the cause of a concern is not always where it seems. A tired look may be caused by a drooping brow, not the eyelids, and a nose may look prominent because the chin sits back. He explains what each option can realistically achieve, and he will say so if a non-surgical treatment or no treatment at all would serve you better.

Safety and honesty run through every step. Your medical history, medicines and lifestyle are reviewed before a date is set, and the main risks of your procedure are discussed in plain language. Recovery is planned around your life too, so if you have a wedding, Eid or an important work commitment coming up, he will help you choose timing that leaves room for swelling to settle.

You can book a consultation through WhatsApp. If you live abroad or in another city, you can begin with photos and a video consultation, then confirm the final plan in person with Dr. Junaid and his team before surgery.

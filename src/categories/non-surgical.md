Not every change needs surgery. Lines that appear when you frown, cheeks that have lost a little volume, skin that looks dull or uneven, or hair that keeps growing back where you do not want it can often be improved with treatments that take an hour or less and let you get on with your day. Non-surgical aesthetics is one of the signature specialties of Dr. Junaid Fateh, a plastic, cosmetic and reconstructive surgeon practising in Lahore.

His surgical background shapes the way he works. Training in plastic surgery, including at the Jinnah Burn and Reconstructive Surgery Centre in Lahore, gives a detailed understanding of facial anatomy, skin healing and how tissues age. That knowledge matters as much for an injection as for an operation. It guides where filler can be placed safely, how much Botox a muscle needs, and which skin treatments suit brown skin without triggering pigmentation.

Every plan starts with a consultation. Dr. Junaid listens to what bothers you, examines your face and skin, and explains honestly what each option can and cannot do. Sometimes the answer is a single treatment; often it is a combination spread over time, such as relaxing expression lines, restoring lost volume and improving skin quality. If surgery would give you a better result, he will say so.

His aim is always a natural look. Most people want to look fresher for a wedding, Eid or a visit home, not different. He prefers measured doses and gradual results that can be built on at review, rather than dramatic changes that are hard to undo. Safety runs through every step, from checking your medical history to making sure you know exactly whom to contact after treatment.

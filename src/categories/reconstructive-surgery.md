Reconstructive surgery is about getting back what an injury, burn, illness or birth difference has taken away. Sometimes that means a hand that can grip again, an eyelid that closes or a neck that turns freely. Sometimes it means a face that feels like your own again after an accident or a cancer operation. Appearance matters in this work, but function and comfort come first.

Hand and reconstructive surgery is one of the signature specialties of Dr. Junaid Fateh, a plastic, cosmetic and reconstructive surgeon practising in Lahore. He holds FCPS in Plastic Surgery from the College of Physicians & Surgeons Pakistan and trained at Jinnah Burn & Reconstructive Surgery Centre in Lahore. Hand injuries and burn scars are a central part of his reconstructive work, alongside facial injuries, skin cancer, cleft lip and palate, and wounds that need grafting.

Every reconstructive plan starts with a careful consultation. Dr. Junaid examines the problem, asks what you need to be able to do at work and at home, and explains the options in plain language, including what surgery can and cannot achieve. Many conditions are best treated in stages, so he sets out a realistic plan with clear priorities. Results vary between people, and he discusses the risks openly before any decision is made.

Recovery is a shared effort. Splints, dressings, scar care and exercises often matter as much as the operation itself, and his team guides you and your family through each step. If you live in another city or abroad, you can begin with a video consultation by sending photos and reports on WhatsApp, with the final plan confirmed in person.

If you have a fresh injury, such as a deep cut, a crushed or cut-off finger, a burn or a facial wound, go to the nearest emergency department first. Once you are safe and stable, Dr. Junaid can see you for specialist repair, follow-up or later reconstruction.

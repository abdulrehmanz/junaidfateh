## About Acne Scar Treatment

Acne usually settles with time, but the marks it leaves can stay for years. When deep or inflamed spots heal, the skin sometimes loses collagen or is pulled down by bands of scar tissue underneath. The result is a pitted, uneven texture that makeup struggles to hide and that shows most clearly when light falls across the face from the side.

Acne scars come in several forms. Ice pick scars are narrow and deep. Boxcar scars are wider, with sharp edges. Rolling scars give the skin a wavy surface because they are tethered to the tissue beneath. Some people also develop raised scars. Flat red or brown marks are a different issue: they are not true scars and often fade on their own or with good skin care.

Dr. Junaid Fateh is a plastic, cosmetic and reconstructive surgeon in Lahore. He treats acne scarring with a combination of procedures matched to the scar types each person has.

## How Dr. Junaid approaches acne scar treatment

The first appointment is about understanding your skin. Dr. Junaid examines your scars under different lighting, identifies which types you have and asks about your acne history, current skin care and any medicines, including isotretinoin. If acne is still active, he will advise on settling it before scar work begins.

Because most people have a mix of scar types, he usually recommends a combined plan rather than a single treatment. One session might focus on releasing tethered scars, while a later one concentrates on resurfacing. The plan is staged so that each step builds on the one before.

South Asian skin can develop dark patches after inflammation, so treatment choice and intensity are adjusted to keep this risk low. Results vary between people, and improvement is gradual over several months. Like any procedure, acne scar treatments carry some risks, such as bruising, infection, pigment change or a temporary increase in redness, and Dr. Junaid goes through these with you at consultation.

## Techniques and options

Options Dr. Junaid may recommend include:

- **Subcision:** A fine needle is passed under the skin to cut the fibrous bands pulling rolling scars down, allowing the surface to lift.
- **Microneedling:** Controlled micro-injuries prompt the skin to make new collagen. It is sometimes combined with PRP (platelet-rich plasma) prepared from your own blood.
- **TCA CROSS:** A concentrated acid is applied precisely into narrow ice pick scars to help them fill in from the base.
- **Punch excision or elevation:** Individual deep scars are removed or raised and closed neatly so they heal flatter.
- **Chemical peels:** Medical peels of a suitable strength improve surface texture and tone over a series of sessions.
- **Laser resurfacing:** For selected skin types, laser treatment can resurface the skin and encourage collagen renewal.
- **Fillers:** Injectable fillers can raise some soft, rolling scars for an instant but temporary improvement.

## What to expect on the day

Most sessions take between 30 minutes and two hours, depending on the methods used and the size of the area. Your face is cleansed and numbed with cream or local anesthetic, and Dr. Junaid then works through the plan for that visit. You go home the same day with clear aftercare advice on cleansing, moisturizing and sun protection, along with a date for your next session.

## About aesthetic gynecology

Aesthetic gynecology covers surgical procedures that address the appearance, comfort and function of the female intimate area. Childbirth, ageing, hormonal changes and natural variation can all play a part. For some women the concern is physical, such as rubbing, irritation or discomfort during exercise. For others it is a loss of support after delivery, or simply feeling self-conscious.

These are sensitive matters, and many women wait a long time before raising them. Dr. Junaid Fateh, a plastic and reconstructive surgeon in Lahore, offers private consultations where you can talk openly and ask questions without feeling judged or rushed.

It is worth knowing that intimate anatomy varies widely, and difference is normal. Surgery is an option for women whose concerns affect their comfort or quality of life, never something anyone should feel pressured into.

## How Dr. Junaid approaches aesthetic gynecology

Your consultation is confidential and unhurried. Dr. Junaid listens to your concerns, asks about your health, pregnancies and deliveries, and carries out an examination only with your consent. You are welcome to bring a female relative or friend with you.

He will explain honestly what each procedure can and cannot achieve. If your symptoms suggest a condition such as prolapse, bladder weakness or a hormonal problem, he may recommend seeing a gynecologist first, as these often need different treatment.

Where surgery is right for you, he plans it to keep the result natural and proportionate and to protect sensation. Timing is discussed too: tightening procedures are usually best left until your family is complete, and surgery is avoided during pregnancy or while breastfeeding. Like any surgery, these procedures carry risks, including bleeding, infection, wound separation, scarring, asymmetry and changes in sensation. Dr. Junaid goes through the risks with you at consultation so you can make the decision in your own time.

## Techniques and options

Options Dr. Junaid may recommend, after a private discussion, include:

- **Labiaplasty:** Reduces or reshapes the inner labia to ease rubbing and discomfort or to correct asymmetry.
- **Labia majora reduction or fat transfer:** Reduces fullness or loose skin of the outer labia, or uses your own fat to restore lost volume.
- **Vaginoplasty:** Tightens the vaginal canal and supporting muscles when they have been stretched by childbirth.
- **Perineoplasty:** Repairs the area between the vagina and anus, often after a tear or episiotomy that has healed poorly.
- **Monsplasty:** Reduces fullness or lifts the area above the pubic bone, sometimes with liposuction.
- **Laser vaginal rejuvenation:** Laser or energy-based treatment of the vaginal tissue, offered to selected women for laxity or dryness after a full assessment, with an honest discussion of the evidence, the possible side effects and the alternatives.
- **Hymenoplasty:** A confidential reconstructive procedure, discussed privately at consultation.

## What to expect on the day

Depending on the procedure, surgery takes one to three hours under local or general anaesthesia. Most women go home the same day, while some stay one night. The clinical team explains your aftercare before you leave, in private.

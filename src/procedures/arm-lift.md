## About arm lift

An arm lift, also called brachioplasty, removes loose, sagging skin and some fat from the underside of the upper arms. It is designed for skin that hangs when you raise your arms, sometimes called "bat wings", which no amount of exercise can tighten.

Loose arm skin usually follows major weight loss, but it can also come with age or simply run in families. Many people find it affects what they wear, especially in long, hot summers when lighter, shorter sleeves are more comfortable. Dr. Junaid Fateh performs arm lifts in Lahore for men and women who want firmer, better-proportioned arms.

An arm lift trades loose skin for a scar. Understanding that trade-off from the start is an important part of deciding whether the operation is right for you.

## How Dr. Junaid approaches arm lift

At consultation, Dr. Junaid looks at how much loose skin you have, how far it extends towards the elbow or onto the chest wall, and how much fat is present. He checks your skin quality and asks about your weight history and general health.

He will show you where the scar is likely to sit and roughly how long it will be. The goal is a smooth, natural taper from armpit to elbow, without making the arm look tight or over-corrected. Where fat is part of the problem, he may combine the lift with liposuction to refine the shape.

Careful technique helps protect the nerves and lymphatic vessels that run along the inner arm. Like any surgery, an arm lift carries risks, including swelling, bleeding, infection, wound separation, numbness and a scar that widens or thickens. Dr. Junaid goes through the risks with you at consultation and explains what you can do to support healing.

## Techniques and options

The right choice depends on how much loose skin you have and where it sits. Options Dr. Junaid may recommend include:

- **Standard arm lift:** A scar along the inner or back of the arm, from the armpit towards the elbow, removes moderate to large amounts of loose skin.
- **Mini arm lift:** For mild looseness near the armpit, a shorter scar hidden in the armpit crease may be enough.
- **Extended arm lift:** The incision continues onto the side of the chest to treat loose skin there too, often after major weight loss.
- **Arm lift with liposuction:** Fat is reduced at the same time for a slimmer, smoother contour.
- **Arm liposuction alone:** When the skin is still firm and the issue is mainly fat, liposuction may give a good result without a long scar.

## What to expect on the day

Surgery usually takes one to three hours under general anaesthesia. You go home the same day wearing compression sleeves, with a family member or friend to help you settle in.

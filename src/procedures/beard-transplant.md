## About beard transplant

A beard transplant moves hair follicles, usually from the back of the scalp, into areas of the face where beard hair is thin or missing. It uses the same FUE method as a scalp transplant, with each graft removed individually, so no strip of skin is cut from the scalp.

Many men grow some beard but are left with gaps on the cheeks, a weak join between moustache and beard, or a sparse chin. Others have bare patches caused by old injuries, acne scarring, burns or a repaired cleft lip. A beard transplant can fill these areas so the beard looks even and connected.

Once the transplanted hairs grow, you can trim, shape or shave them like the rest of your beard. Scalp hair can feel a little softer or straighter than natural beard hair, but placed at the right angle and density it usually blends in well.

## How Dr. Junaid approaches beard transplant

Facial hair grows at a very flat angle and changes direction across the cheeks, jaw, chin and neck. Getting this right is what separates a natural beard from an obviously transplanted one, so Dr. Junaid Fateh maps the direction of your existing hair and plans each zone separately. Single-hair grafts are used along the cheek line and edges, with slightly larger grafts where more density is needed.

At consultation he asks how you like to wear your beard, from short stubble to a fuller style, and draws the planned shape on your face so you can see it in the mirror before anything is done. He also checks your scalp donor hair, because using it for the beard should not leave you short if you want a scalp transplant later.

Because he also works in reconstructive surgery, Dr. Junaid is used to assessing scarred skin. Scars can have a poorer blood supply, so grafts may take less reliably and a second session is sometimes planned. He explains this, along with other risks such as redness, swelling, infection, ingrown hairs and uneven growth, at your consultation.

## Techniques and options

Options Dr. Junaid may recommend include:

- **Full beard design:** building a beard from very little existing hair, with the shape agreed in advance and density added in stages if needed.
- **Patchy area fill:** adding grafts only to the gaps so the whole beard looks even.
- **Moustache and goatee:** creating or thickening hair around the mouth and chin.
- **Cheek line and sideburns:** defining the upper border of the beard and joining it to the sideburns.
- **Scar camouflage:** placing hair into facial scars, including old cleft lip repair scars on the upper lip.

## What to expect on the day

The hair over a small donor patch is clipped short, then local anaesthetic is used to numb the scalp and the face. After harvesting, tiny sites are made on the face at the planned angles and each graft is placed individually. The procedure usually takes 3–6 hours with breaks, and you go home the same day.

## About body contouring after weight loss

Losing a large amount of weight, whether through bariatric surgery or years of steady effort, is a real achievement. Yet skin that has been stretched for a long time often cannot shrink back. Many people are left with folds on the tummy, back, arms, thighs or chest that hide their new shape and can be uncomfortable to live with.

Body contouring after weight loss is a group of operations that remove this excess skin and reshape the tissues underneath. Dr. Junaid Fateh plans these procedures as a whole, looking at your body from head to toe rather than at one area in isolation.

Hanging skin is not only a cosmetic concern. In hot, humid summers, deep folds trap sweat, leading to rashes, chafing, odour and repeated skin infections. They can also make walking, exercise and finding clothes that fit harder than they should be.

## How Dr. Junaid approaches body contouring after weight loss

Your first consultation covers your weight-loss journey, how long your weight has been stable, your general health and any nutritional issues, particularly after bariatric surgery. Dr. Junaid examines each area and asks which concerns bother you most, because this usually decides where to start.

Most people need more than one operation. Dr. Junaid groups procedures into safe stages, a few months apart, so each surgery stays within a sensible length and you have time to recover fully in between. Scars are planned along natural lines and, wherever possible, under clothing.

Good preparation lowers risk. You may need blood tests to check iron, protein and vitamin levels, and you will need help from family at home during the first weeks. Like any surgery, these operations carry risks, including wound healing problems, fluid collection, infection, blood clots and scars that heal less neatly than hoped. Dr. Junaid goes through the risks with you at consultation and explains how each one is managed.

## Techniques and options

Your plan is built from one or more of the following. Options Dr. Junaid may recommend include:

- **Lower body lift (belt lipectomy):** Removes a band of loose skin around the waist, lifting the tummy, hips, outer thighs and buttocks in one operation.
- **Panniculectomy:** Removes the hanging apron of skin from the lower tummy, mainly to relieve rashes and discomfort.
- **Tummy tuck:** Removes loose skin, repairs separated muscles and creates a flatter front. A fleur-de-lis pattern may be used when the skin is very loose.
- **Arm lift:** Removes sagging skin from the upper arms, sometimes extending onto the side of the chest.
- **Thigh lift:** Tightens loose skin on the inner thighs, which often causes chafing when you walk.
- **Breast lift or male chest contouring:** Reshapes the chest after volume loss and skin stretching.

## What to expect on the day

Each stage is done under general anaesthesia and can take four to eight hours, depending on what is included. You will usually stay two or three nights while the clinical team manages pain, checks drains and helps you get up and walk.

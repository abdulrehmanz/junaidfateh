## About Botox Injections

Each time you frown, narrow your eyes against bright sun or lift your brows, small muscles in your face contract and fold the skin above them. Over the years those folds can turn into lines that stay visible even when your face is relaxed. Botox, a purified form of botulinum toxin, calms the signal from the nerve to those muscles, so they relax and the skin over them smooths out.

At his clinics in Lahore, Dr. Junaid Fateh, a plastic, cosmetic and reconstructive surgeon, uses Botox to soften expression lines on the upper face and to treat a few other concerns, such as an overactive jaw muscle or heavy underarm sweating. The effect is temporary, which many people find reassuring: if you like the result you can repeat it, and if you do not, it wears off.

Botox does not add volume or lift sagging skin. Deep creases that are present at rest, hollow cheeks or loose skin usually need a different approach, such as dermal fillers or a surgical option, and Dr. Junaid will tell you honestly which one fits your concern.

## How Dr. Junaid approaches Botox

Your appointment starts with a conversation. Dr. Junaid asks what bothers you, then watches how your face moves as you frown, smile and raise your brows. Muscle strength varies a great deal from person to person, and even between the two sides of one face, so the dose and injection points are planned for you rather than taken from a chart.

His aim is a face that looks rested, not one that has stopped moving. He prefers to begin with a measured dose and fine-tune it at a review rather than overdo it at the first visit. Safety comes first too: he checks your medical history and medicines, and explains the possible side effects before any treatment begins.

## Techniques and options

- **Frown lines:** Relaxes the muscles between the brows that create vertical lines and a cross or tired look.
- **Forehead lines:** Softens horizontal lines while leaving enough movement to stop the brows feeling heavy.
- **Crow's feet:** Smooths the fan of lines at the outer corners of the eyes that shows when you smile.
- **Bunny lines:** Treats the small creases on the sides of the nose that appear when you scrunch it.
- **Jaw slimming:** Gradually reduces the bulk of an enlarged chewing (masseter) muscle for a softer lower face, and may ease clenching.
- **Gummy smile:** A tiny dose above the upper lip can reduce how much gum shows when you smile.
- **Neck bands:** Softens prominent vertical cords at the front of the neck.
- **Underarm sweating:** Reduces sweat production for people with excessive sweating, which many find helpful through Lahore's long, humid summers.
- **Blepharospasm:** Small doses around the eyes can calm involuntary blinking or eyelid spasm, usually alongside assessment by an eye specialist or neurologist.
- **Facial tics and spasms:** Selected muscle twitches, such as hemifacial spasm, may be eased with careful injections after the cause has been checked.

## What to expect on the day

Your skin is cleansed, and numbing cream is applied if you would like it. Dr. Junaid marks the injection points and uses a very fine needle, and the injections themselves take a few minutes. You can usually drive, return to work and carry on as normal, following the simple aftercare advice the team gives you.

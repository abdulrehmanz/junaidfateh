## About Brazilian butt lift

A Brazilian butt lift, usually shortened to BBL, reshapes the buttocks using your own fat rather than an implant. Fat is gently removed by liposuction from areas such as the tummy, flanks, back or thighs, then purified and carefully transferred to the buttocks and hips.

Because it combines two steps, a BBL changes your shape in two places at once. It slims the donor areas while adding fullness and curve where you want it, with the aim of a balanced, natural silhouette that suits your frame.

Dr. Junaid Fateh offers BBL in Lahore with a strong emphasis on safety and privacy. Consultations are discreet, and you can talk through your goals openly, without any pressure to go ahead.

## How Dr. Junaid approaches Brazilian butt lift

Safety comes first. International plastic surgery guidance now recommends that fat for a BBL is placed only in the fatty layer beneath the skin, above the buttock muscle, because injecting into or under the muscle is linked to rare but serious complications. Dr. Junaid plans BBL around this safety-focused approach and keeps the volume transferred within sensible limits for your body.

Careful patient selection is just as important. At consultation, Dr. Junaid checks your general health, your weight, how much spare fat you have and the quality of your skin. Very slim people may not have enough fat to transfer, while a high body weight or certain medical conditions can raise the risks. If a BBL is not right for you, he will say so.

He also listens closely to what you would like to achieve. Rather than chasing size, he focuses on proportion, so your waist, hips and buttocks look natural together. Like any surgery, a BBL carries risks, including infection, fluid collection, lumps, unevenness and, rarely, serious fat-related complications. Dr. Junaid goes through the risks with you at consultation, so nothing comes as a surprise.

## Techniques and options

A BBL is planned in several linked steps. Options Dr. Junaid may recommend include:

- **Fat harvesting:** Liposuction removes fat from areas you would like slimmer, which also helps define the waist.
- **Fat processing:** The collected fat is cleaned and prepared so that healthy fat cells are used for transfer.
- **Placement above the muscle:** Fat is placed in the layer beneath the skin, not into the muscle, to shape the buttocks safely.
- **Hip and waist shaping:** Fat can also be used to soften hip dips, while liposuction around the waist adds definition.
- **Staged top-up:** If more volume is needed, a second, smaller session can be planned once the first result has settled.

## What to expect on the day

Surgery lasts three to four hours under general anaesthesia. Afterwards, the clinical team fits your compression garment and shows you how to lie and move without pressing on your buttocks. Depending on your recovery, you go home the same day or after one night.

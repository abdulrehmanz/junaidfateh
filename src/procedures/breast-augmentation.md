## About Breast Augmentation

Breast augmentation, also called augmentation mammoplasty, increases the size and fullness of the breasts, most often with silicone or saline implants. Some women have always felt their breasts were small for their figure. Others find their breasts have flattened or emptied after having children, breastfeeding or losing weight, or have one side that is clearly smaller than the other.

Dr. Junaid Fateh, a plastic and cosmetic surgeon in Lahore, sees breast augmentation as a private and personal decision. His goal is a shape that suits your body and looks like a natural version of you, not a size picked from a chart.

There is no need to rush. You are welcome to take time to think, talk it over with your husband or family, and come back with more questions before you decide.

## How Dr. Junaid approaches breast augmentation

Your consultation begins with a conversation about what you would like to change and why. Dr. Junaid then measures your chest width, the base of each breast, how much your skin stretches and how thick your natural tissue is, because these set the limits for an implant that will look and feel right. You are welcome to bring a family member or friend with you.

Instead of focusing on cup letters, he matches the width and projection of the implant to your frame, so it sits in proportion with your shoulders and hips and moves naturally. He will tell you honestly if a very large implant is likely to look artificial or strain your tissues over time.

Safety comes first. You have a health check before surgery, and Dr. Junaid explains the risks, which include bleeding, infection, hardening of the scar tissue around the implant (capsular contracture), implant leak or rupture, visible rippling, changes in nipple sensation, asymmetry and the chance of further surgery later in life.

## Techniques and options

Depending on your anatomy and goals, options Dr. Junaid may recommend include:

- **Silicone gel implants:** soft and natural to the touch, and the most widely used type, with regular check-ups over the years.
- **Saline implants:** filled with sterile salt water once in place, which can allow a slightly shorter incision, though they may feel firmer.
- **Round or teardrop shape:** round implants give more fullness at the top of the breast, while teardrop (anatomical) implants follow a gentler slope.
- **Above or below the muscle:** placing the implant partly under the chest muscle gives extra cover in slimmer women, while placement above it can suit those with more natural tissue.
- **Incision choice:** usually a short cut hidden in the crease under the breast, or occasionally at the border of the areola.
- **Fat transfer or composite augmentation:** your own fat can be used for a modest increase, or added around an implant to soften its edges.

## What to expect on the day

You come in having fasted, Dr. Junaid confirms the plan and marks your chest, and the operation takes roughly an hour or two under general anaesthesia. Most women go home the same day or after one night, wearing a support bra, with a companion to take them home.

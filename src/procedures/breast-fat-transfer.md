## About Breast Augmentation with Fat Transfer

Fat transfer breast augmentation, also called lipofilling or fat grafting, uses your own fat instead of an implant to add fullness to the breasts. Fat is taken by gentle liposuction from an area such as the tummy, flanks or thighs, prepared, and then placed into the breasts in small amounts.

It suits women who want a modest, soft increase in size and would prefer not to have an implant. Because the fat becomes part of your own tissue, the result feels natural to the touch, and the donor area is often a little slimmer afterwards.

Dr. Junaid Fateh is open with every patient about what fat transfer can and cannot do. Not all the fat survives, the size increase is limited by how much your skin can safely hold, and some women need a second session to reach their goal.

## How Dr. Junaid approaches breast fat transfer

At consultation, Dr. Junaid discusses the change you hope for, examines the shape and skin of your breasts, and checks which areas have enough spare fat. Very slim women may not have enough to harvest. He asks about your personal and family history of breast problems and, depending on your age, may suggest breast imaging before surgery so there is a clear baseline.

During the operation the fat is placed in many thin layers rather than in one pocket. This gives each tiny portion a good chance of picking up a blood supply, which helps more of it survive and lowers the risk of lumps. He avoids overfilling, because packing in too much fat at once can reduce how much takes.

Risks include loss of some of the grafted volume, small firm lumps or oil cysts, an uneven result, infection and irregularities at the donor site. Dr. Junaid explains each of these carefully at your consultation.

## Techniques and options

Depending on your goals and body shape, options Dr. Junaid may recommend include:

- **Gentle fat harvesting:** low-pressure liposuction through openings of a few millimetres, designed to keep fat cells intact.
- **Fat preparation:** the harvested fat is cleaned and separated from fluid and oil so that only healthy fat is used.
- **Layered micro-injection:** tiny amounts are placed through many fine passes at different depths within the breast.
- **Staged sessions:** a second session after several months, once the first result has settled, for women who would like a little more.
- **Composite augmentation:** an implant provides the main volume while a layer of fat over it disguises its outline, especially in the upper breast.
- **Fat transfer with a lift:** combined with a breast lift when the breasts have dropped as well as lost volume.

## What to expect on the day

After fasting as instructed, you meet Dr. Junaid, who marks both the donor areas and the breasts. The procedure takes about two to three hours, either under general anaesthesia or with sedation and local numbing, and you can usually leave the same day in a compression garment.

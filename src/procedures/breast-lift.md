## About Breast Lift Surgery

A breast lift, known medically as mastopexy, raises breasts that have dropped and reshapes them into a firmer, more youthful contour. Stretched skin is removed, the inner support of the breast is tightened, and the nipple and areola are moved to a higher, forward-facing position.

Breasts naturally descend over time. Pregnancy, breastfeeding, significant weight loss, ageing and genetics all play a part, and many mothers notice the change once their children are older. A lift alters shape and position more than size, which is why some women choose to combine it with added volume.

Dr. Junaid Fateh understands that this is a deeply personal subject for many women. Your consultation with him in Lahore is private and unhurried, so you can talk openly about how your body has changed and what you would like to feel again.

## How Dr. Junaid approaches breast lift surgery

At consultation, Dr. Junaid looks at where the nipple sits in relation to the crease under the breast, how much loose skin there is and whether you have enough volume for the shape you want. These details decide the type of lift and the length of the scars.

His approach is to use the shortest scar that will still give a good, lasting shape. A very small incision may not hold a significant lift, and he will explain that trade-off honestly. The aim is natural, rounded breasts that suit your age and frame, rather than an artificially high position.

Like any surgery, a lift carries risks, including scarring, changes in nipple sensation, asymmetry, delayed healing and some gradual settling over the years. Smoking slows healing, so it should ideally be stopped well before surgery. Dr. Junaid goes through all of this with you at consultation.

## Techniques and options

Options Dr. Junaid may recommend include:

- **Crescent lift:** a small half-moon of skin is removed above the areola, suitable only for very mild drooping.
- **Periareolar (doughnut) lift:** a scar around the areola only, useful for mild drooping and for reducing a stretched areola.
- **Vertical (lollipop) lift:** a scar around the areola and down to the crease, giving a reliable lift for moderate drooping.
- **Anchor (inverted-T) lift:** adds a scar along the crease for marked drooping and a large amount of loose skin.
- **Lift with implant or fat transfer:** restores upper fullness at the same time; in some cases the two are planned as separate stages for safety.

## What to expect on the day

On the morning of surgery you come in without having eaten, and Dr. Junaid marks your breasts while you are upright. Surgery usually takes two to three hours under general anaesthesia. You go home the same day or the following morning in a support bra, with someone to accompany you.

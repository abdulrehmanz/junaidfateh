## About Breast Reduction

Breast reduction, or reduction mammoplasty, removes extra breast tissue, fat and skin to make the breasts smaller, lighter and better shaped. For many women, very large breasts are a physical burden rather than a cosmetic concern. Aching shoulders and upper back, deep bra-strap grooves, rashes under the breast fold in the heat of a Pakistani summer and difficulty exercising are common complaints.

Dr. Junaid Fateh, a plastic surgeon in Lahore, sees breast reduction as surgery that can improve daily comfort as well as appearance. The operation also lifts the breasts and moves the nipple and areola higher, so the result is a smaller breast with a firmer, more youthful shape.

It can be an emotional subject too. Some women have felt self-conscious since their teens or have struggled for years to find clothes that fit. Whatever your reasons, the consultation is a private space to talk openly.

## How Dr. Junaid approaches breast reduction

Dr. Junaid starts by asking about your symptoms, how they affect your life and the size you hope to reach. Bra sizes vary between brands, so he relies on examination and measurements rather than cup letters. He checks the position of the nipple, the quality of your skin and whether one side is larger, and depending on your age and history he may ask for a breast scan before surgery.

The plan aims for breasts that suit your frame, neither too small nor still heavy. The nipple is normally kept attached to a bridge of underlying tissue to protect its blood supply and, where possible, its sensation.

Risks include bleeding, infection, slow wound healing, noticeable scars, changes in nipple sensation, uneven size or shape and, rarely, problems with the blood supply to the nipple. Smoking increases several of these risks. Dr. Junaid goes through them with you at consultation.

## Techniques and options

The right technique depends on how much tissue needs removing and how much loose skin you have. Options Dr. Junaid may recommend include:

- **Vertical (lollipop) reduction:** a scar around the areola and straight down to the crease, suited to small and moderate reductions.
- **Anchor (inverted-T) reduction:** adds a scar along the crease under the breast, giving more control for larger reductions and looser skin.
- **Liposuction-assisted reduction:** a smaller reduction for selected women with mainly fatty breasts and firm skin, with very small scars but limited lift.
- **Free nipple graft:** for very large reductions, the nipple and areola may be removed and replaced higher up as a graft. This affects sensation and breastfeeding, so it is used only when it is safer for the nipple.
- **Side-chest contouring:** liposuction of fullness near the armpit can be added to refine the overall outline.

## What to expect on the day

You are admitted having fasted, your markings are drawn while you are standing, and surgery takes around two to four hours under general anaesthesia. You usually stay one night and go home the next day in a support bra.

## About brow and forehead lifts

Your eyebrows frame your eyes and play a large part in how others read your mood. With time, the forehead tissues loosen and the brows slowly drop, especially at their outer ends. This can create a heavy, hooded look over the eyes, deeper lines across the forehead and a frown that seems to stay even when you are relaxed.

A brow lift, also called a forehead lift, raises the brows to a more natural height and can soften lines across the forehead and between the eyebrows. Dr. Junaid Fateh offers brow lift surgery in Lahore for women and men who want a fresher, more open upper face.

Many people who believe they need eyelid surgery actually have a low brow pressing down on the upper lid. Treating the eyelid alone in that situation can pull the brow even lower, so getting the diagnosis right is the first and most important step.

## How Dr. Junaid approaches brow and forehead lifts

At your consultation, Dr. Junaid studies the position of your brows when relaxed and when raised, the amount of upper eyelid skin, the height of your forehead and the shape of your hairline. A high forehead or receding hairline affects which technique he suggests, because some approaches move the hairline slightly.

He plans the amount of lift with care. Over-lifting is avoided, as it can leave a permanently startled expression, and the shape of the brow is matched to your face and gender. He also considers whether the muscles that cause frown lines need softening as part of the operation.

Your health, medicines and smoking history are reviewed to reduce the chance of complications. Like any surgery, a brow lift has risks, including bleeding, infection, numbness of the scalp that is usually temporary, hair thinning along the incision, asymmetry and, rarely, weakness of the forehead muscles. Dr. Junaid talks these through with you plainly before you decide.

## Techniques and options

The main approaches Dr. Junaid may recommend are:

- **Endoscopic brow lift:** Several short incisions hidden in the hair allow the brow to be lifted from underneath, with small scars and a relatively quick recovery.
- **Temporal brow lift:** Small incisions in the hair near the temples lift the outer third of the brow, which is where drooping is most common.
- **Hairline forehead lift:** An incision along the front of the hairline lifts the brow without making a high forehead look taller.
- **Direct brow lift:** Skin is removed just above the eyebrow for precise lift. It leaves a scar along the brow, so it is mostly used for older patients or after facial nerve weakness.
- **Non-surgical brow lift:** Anti-wrinkle injections or threads can give a small, temporary lift for early changes.
- **Coronal brow lift:** An incision across the top of the scalp, well behind the hairline, allows a strong, even lift of the whole forehead. It suits heavier brows and people with a normal or low hairline.

## What to expect on the day

A brow lift usually takes one to two hours under general anaesthesia or local anaesthetic with sedation, depending on the technique. It is generally a day case, so you return home that evening with a light dressing and clear instructions for the first night. Ask a family member to drive you.

## About Cheek Implants (Malar Augmentation)

The cheekbones give the face much of its shape. When they are naturally flat, or when the mid-face loses volume with age, the face can look tired or less defined, and the nose or jaw may appear more prominent than you would like.

Cheek implants, also known as malar augmentation, are shaped implants, most often made of solid silicone, placed over the cheekbone to add projection. They come in several shapes and sizes. Some sit directly on the cheekbone to make it stand out more, while others sit lower to fill hollows beneath it.

Dr. Junaid Fateh, a plastic, cosmetic and reconstructive surgeon in Lahore, offers cheek implants alongside fat grafting and non-surgical options, so you can compare the choices properly before you decide.

## How Dr. Junaid approaches cheek implants

The consultation looks at your face as a whole. Dr. Junaid examines your cheekbones from several angles, how they relate to your eyes, nose and jaw, and whether the issue is bone structure, soft-tissue volume or a mix of both. This helps decide whether an implant, fat grafting or filler would give the most natural result.

If an implant is the right choice, he selects its type, size and position to suit your anatomy. Modest changes often look the most natural. Most faces are slightly different on each side, so careful measurement and placement help both cheeks look balanced. He will explain where the incisions go, usually inside the mouth, and how to look after them while they heal.

Like any surgery, cheek implants carry risks. These include infection, swelling, implant movement, asymmetry, temporary numbness of the cheek or upper lip and, occasionally, the need for a further operation to adjust or remove an implant. Dr. Junaid discusses each of these at your consultation, along with practical steps that lower them, such as careful oral hygiene and not smoking.

## Techniques and options

- **Malar implants:** Sit on the cheekbone itself to increase height and projection for a more sculpted look.
- **Submalar implants:** Sit just below the cheekbone to fill hollows and soften a gaunt mid-face.
- **Combined implants:** Cover both areas for people who need projection and mid-face volume together.
- **Fat grafting to the cheeks:** Uses your own fat instead of an implant, suited to softer, more gradual volume restoration.
- **Cheek fillers:** A non-surgical, temporary option for subtle enhancement or for previewing a result before surgery.

## What to expect on the day

Cheek implant surgery usually takes one to two hours under general anesthesia or local anesthesia with sedation. Dr. Junaid marks your face, places the implants through small incisions inside the mouth and closes them with dissolving stitches. Most people go home the same day with clear aftercare instructions and a follow-up appointment.

Because you will have had sedation or general anesthesia, arrange for someone to take you home and stay with you for the first night. Have soft foods, mouthwash and an extra pillow ready before the day, so you can rest comfortably while the early swelling settles.

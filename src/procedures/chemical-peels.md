## About Chemical Peels

A chemical peel is a carefully controlled exfoliation. A solution based on a medical-grade acid is applied to the skin for a set time. It loosens and removes the outer layers of dull, damaged or pigmented cells, and the skin that replaces them is smoother and more even. Stronger peels also stimulate collagen in the layer beneath.

Peels are a useful tool for many common concerns in Pakistan: uneven tone and dark patches made worse by strong sun, marks left behind by acne, oily and congested skin, and early fine lines. Dr. Junaid Fateh offers medical chemical peels at his clinics in Lahore and Layyah, from gentle refreshing peels to medium-depth treatments.

A peel is not one single product. The type of acid, its strength and how long it stays on all change the result and the recovery, which is why a medical assessment matters before the first application. In Lahore's long, bright summers, stronger peels are often planned for the cooler months, when healing skin is easier to protect from the sun, although light peels can be done year-round with good sunscreen habits.

## How Dr. Junaid approaches chemical peels

Brown skin responds well to peels, but it is also more prone to post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation, the dark marks that can follow irritation. Dr. Junaid starts by identifying your skin type and the cause of your concern, since melasma, acne marks and sun spots each need a different plan.

He then chooses a peel strong enough to help but gentle enough for your skin, and often asks you to prepare with a suitable cream for a few weeks beforehand. He prefers to build results over a series of lighter peels rather than risk one aggressive treatment. Throughout, he stresses daily sun protection, because without it pigmentation tends to return.

## Techniques and options

- **Light peels:** Gentle acids such as glycolic, lactic or mandelic acid to brighten skin and smooth texture with little downtime.
- **Salicylic acid peels:** Oil-soluble peels that clear pores, useful for acne-prone and oily skin.
- **Combination peels:** Blends of several acids, such as Jessner's solution, for acne marks, pigmentation and texture.
- **Medium-depth peels:** TCA (trichloroacetic acid) peels for more noticeable improvement in sun damage, fine lines and some scarring, with a week or so of peeling.
- **Pigmentation-focused peels:** Formulations selected for melasma and uneven tone, used alongside home skincare and sun protection.

## What to expect on the day

Your skin is cleansed to remove oil, and the peel is applied in even layers while its effect on your skin is watched closely. It is then neutralised or removed. The appointment takes 15 to 30 minutes, and you leave with sunscreen applied and written aftercare advice. For the first few days, skip make-up if you can, keep your skin moisturised and wear sunscreen every morning, even indoors near windows. The team will tell you when your next peel can safely be scheduled.

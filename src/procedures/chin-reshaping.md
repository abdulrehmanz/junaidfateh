## About chin reshaping

The chin is easy to overlook, yet it anchors the lower face. A chin that sits too far back can make the nose look larger, the neck look softer and the profile look unfinished. A chin that is too long or too prominent can dominate the face instead. Small changes in its size and position often create a surprisingly large improvement in overall balance.

Chin reshaping, also called mentoplasty, covers several different procedures. The chin can be brought forward with an implant, moved by repositioning the bone (genioplasty), reduced, or enhanced temporarily with filler. Dr. Junaid Fateh, who practises plastic and cosmetic surgery in Lahore, offers each of these, and choosing between them is a big part of your consultation.

Both men and women ask about chin reshaping. Men often want a stronger, squarer jawline, while women more often want a softer, well-defined chin that balances the lips and nose.

## How Dr. Junaid approaches chin reshaping

Dr. Junaid looks at your face as a whole. He studies your profile, the relationship between your nose, lips and chin, the length of your lower face and the symmetry of your jaw. He also checks your bite and teeth, because a chin that looks weak is sometimes part of a jaw that sits back, which needs a different solution.

Photographs help you both see the profile clearly and agree a realistic goal. The aim is a chin that suits your features and looks as if it belongs to you, not one that seems added on. In some cases he may suggest trying filler first, so you can see how a change in projection feels before committing to surgery.

Safety is central. Your general health, oral hygiene and smoking history are reviewed, and the operation is planned to protect the nerves that give feeling to the lower lip and chin. Like any surgery, chin reshaping has risks, including infection, implant movement, asymmetry, numbness that is usually temporary and the chance that a later adjustment is needed. Dr. Junaid discusses each of these openly before you commit.

## Techniques and options

Options Dr. Junaid may discuss with you include:

- **Chin implant:** A shaped medical-grade implant is placed over the chin bone through a small incision inside the lower lip or under the chin, increasing forward projection.
- **Sliding genioplasty:** The lower edge of the chin bone is cut, moved forward, back, up, down or sideways, and fixed in its new position. It suits larger or more complex changes.
- **Chin reduction:** Bone is reshaped or repositioned to reduce a chin that is too long or too prominent.
- **Combined profile surgery:** Chin work can be paired with rhinoplasty, or with liposuction under the chin to sharpen the jawline.
- **Non-surgical chin enhancement:** Dermal filler adds projection and definition without surgery. The effect is temporary and needs top-ups.

## What to expect on the day

Expect to spend one to two hours in theatre. Depending on the technique, you will either be fully asleep or relaxed under sedation with local anaesthetic. It is normally a day case, and you leave with a supportive dressing, advice on a soft diet, and instructions for keeping your mouth clean while the incision heals.

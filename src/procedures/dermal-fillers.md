## About Dermal Fillers

With time, the face slowly loses fat, bone support and some of the natural moisture-holding substances in the skin. Cheeks flatten, hollows appear under the eyes, and folds deepen around the nose and mouth. Dermal fillers are soft gels placed beneath the skin to replace some of that lost volume and support, or to add shape where you would like more definition.

Most facial fillers today are made from hyaluronic acid, a sugar-based molecule your skin already produces. It holds water, feels natural once settled, and is gradually broken down by the body over months. Because it can also be dissolved with an enzyme, it offers a margin of safety that permanent fillers do not.

Dr. Junaid Fateh offers filler treatments at his Lahore clinic as part of his work in non-surgical aesthetics. Treatment is quick and the difference is visible straight away, but it is still a medical procedure that depends on detailed knowledge of facial anatomy.

## How Dr. Junaid approaches dermal fillers

Good filler work starts by looking at the whole face, not just the line that worries you. A deep fold beside the mouth, for example, is often a sign of lost cheek support higher up, and restoring the cheek can soften the fold more naturally than filling the fold itself. Dr. Junaid studies your face from the front and side, talks through your goals, and explains what filler can and cannot achieve.

He favours a measured approach, especially for lips and for first-time patients. It is easy to add a little more at a review, and far better than an overfilled result. He chooses between a fine needle and a blunt cannula depending on the area, injects slowly with close attention to known blood vessels, and makes sure you know the warning signs to watch for afterwards.

## Techniques and options

- **Cheeks:** Restores midface volume and gives gentle support to the lower face.
- **Lips:** Adds shape, definition or subtle volume, aiming for lips that still suit your face.
- **Under-eye hollows:** Softens a tired, sunken look in carefully selected patients, since not every dark circle is caused by volume loss.
- **Smile lines and marionette lines:** Softens folds around the nose and mouth, often combined with cheek support.
- **Chin and jawline:** Improves chin projection and jaw definition for better balance in profile.
- **Temples:** Fills hollowing at the sides of the forehead that can make the face look drawn.
- **Hands:** Restores volume to the backs of the hands so tendons and veins look less prominent.

## What to expect on the day

Photos are taken, numbing cream is applied and the skin is cleaned thoroughly. Treatment usually takes 15 to 45 minutes depending on the areas. You leave with written aftercare advice and a direct way to reach the clinical team if anything concerns you.

## About eyebrow transplant

An eyebrow transplant uses the FUE method to take fine hairs, one by one, from the back or sides of the scalp and place them into the brows. It can fill thin or patchy brows, rebuild ones that have been over-plucked or threaded away over many years, or bring hair back to a scar from an old injury.

Because the hair is real and grows from your own follicles, it gives a softer, more natural look than pencil or microblading. It does need some upkeep. Scalp hair grows longer than brow hair, so the transplanted hairs usually need regular trimming, and some people use a little brow gel to keep them lying flat.

Women and men both ask for this procedure, often with different goals. Many women want a defined arch and a neat, shaped brow, while men usually prefer a straighter, fuller and less groomed look.

## How Dr. Junaid approaches eyebrow transplant

Eyebrows are a small area, but they change the balance of the whole face, so planning takes time. Dr. Junaid Fateh looks at your face shape, brow bone and the position of your eyes, then draws the new brows on with you before surgery. You see the proposed shape in the mirror and it is adjusted until it looks right for you rather than for a passing trend.

Brow hairs lie almost flat against the skin and change direction along the brow. Those at the inner end point upwards, while those along the body and tail run outwards and slightly down. Dr. Junaid uses mostly single-hair grafts placed at very shallow angles to copy this pattern, which is the detail that makes a transplanted brow look natural.

He also checks why the brows are thin. Loss linked to thyroid problems, alopecia areata or certain skin conditions may need medical treatment first, and a transplant is not advised while such a condition is active. He goes through the risks with you at consultation, including swelling around the eyes, infection, ingrown hairs, uneven growth and hairs growing at an unwanted angle.

## Techniques and options

Options Dr. Junaid may recommend include:

- **Full brow reconstruction:** rebuilding an entire eyebrow where very little hair remains.
- **Density filling:** adding hairs between existing ones to make thin brows look fuller.
- **Shape and arch correction:** extending the tail, filling a gap or adjusting the arch for better balance.
- **Scar coverage:** placing hair into scars from injury or earlier surgery, sometimes over two sessions.
- **Brow design to suit you:** a softer arched shape or a straighter, heavier brow, depending on your features and preference.

## What to expect on the day

A small patch of hair at the back of the scalp is trimmed, usually where longer hair above it can cover the area. The brows and the donor site are numbed with local anaesthetic. Grafts are taken and then placed one at a time along the agreed design. The procedure usually takes 2–4 hours, and you go home the same day.

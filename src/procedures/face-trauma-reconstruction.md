## About Face Trauma Reconstruction

Facial injuries are common in Pakistan. Road traffic accidents, motorbike falls, workplace incidents, sports, assaults and dog bites are among the usual causes. The face holds the eyes, nose and mouth, along with the nerves and muscles of expression, so even a moderate injury can affect vision, breathing, eating, speech and how you feel about your appearance.

Face trauma reconstruction covers the repair of cuts and torn tissue, broken facial bones, and injuries to the eyelids, lips, ears and nose. It also includes later surgery to improve scars or deformities from injuries that did not heal well.

Dr. Junaid Fateh is a plastic and reconstructive surgeon with FCPS in Plastic Surgery who repairs facial injuries with the precision a visible area demands. If an injury has just happened, your first stop should always be the nearest emergency department, where head, eye and airway injuries can be checked and treated.

## How Dr. Junaid approaches facial injuries

Dr. Junaid begins with a careful examination. He checks how your eyes move, whether your bite feels normal, how each part of your face moves and where feeling has been lost. Scans are reviewed so that every fracture is understood before surgery, and photographs help with planning and later comparison.

For cuts, the aim is a scar that is as fine as possible. The wound is cleaned thoroughly, damaged edges are trimmed conservatively, and key landmarks such as the edge of the lip, the eyelid margin and the eyebrow are lined up exactly before the layers are closed. Where tissue is missing, he uses nearby skin or a graft to rebuild it.

For fractures, he restores the shape and position of the bones so your face looks balanced and works properly. Small plates and screws may hold the bones in place, placed through incisions hidden inside the mouth, in the hairline or along natural creases wherever possible.

He is honest that results vary between people and depend on the original injury. Like any surgery, facial reconstruction carries risks, and Dr. Junaid goes through them with you at consultation.

## Techniques and options

Options Dr. Junaid may recommend include:

- **Layered laceration repair:** Precise closure of facial cuts, including those on the lip, eyelid, ear and nose.
- **Nasal fracture realignment:** Straightening a broken nose, either by manipulation or through small incisions, to restore shape and breathing.
- **Cheekbone and eye socket repair:** Lifting and fixing a displaced cheekbone or rebuilding the floor of the eye socket to correct a sunken eye or double vision.
- **Jaw fracture repair:** Restoring a normal bite with plates or wiring, sometimes alongside a dental specialist.
- **Local flaps and grafts:** Replacing lost skin or tissue with nearby skin or a graft for a close match.
- **Facial nerve repair:** Rejoining a cut branch of the facial nerve, ideally soon after injury, to give movement the best chance to return.
- **Secondary reconstruction:** Scar revision, nose correction or contour work for injuries that healed with a deformity.

## What to expect on the day

Fracture repairs are done under general anaesthetic, and you may stay in hospital for one to five days depending on the injury. You wake with some swelling and, after nose surgery, possibly a small splint. The team explains wound care, diet and pain relief before you go home.

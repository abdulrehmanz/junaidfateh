## About facelift surgery

As the years pass, the soft tissues of the face slowly descend. Cheeks lose their lift, jowls form along the jaw, the folds between the nose and mouth deepen and the neck can loosen. Skincare, fillers and threads can help in the early stages, but once tissue has genuinely dropped, a facelift is usually the most effective way to lift it back into place.

A facelift, or rhytidectomy, repositions the deeper supporting layer of the face and neck and removes the extra skin that has stretched over time. Dr. Junaid Fateh, a plastic and cosmetic surgeon in Lahore, performs facelifts with one clear aim: you should look rested and like yourself, not as though you have had work done.

It helps to know what a facelift does and does not treat. It works on the middle and lower face and, very often, the neck. It does not change your brow, eyelids or skin texture on its own, which is why it is sometimes combined with other procedures.

## How Dr. Junaid approaches a facelift

Planning starts with a detailed conversation about what you see in the mirror and what you would like to change. Dr. Junaid examines your skin quality, the degree of sagging in the cheeks, jowls and neck, your bone structure and your hairline, because these decide where scars can be hidden.

He also reviews your general health carefully. Blood pressure, diabetes, blood-thinning medicines and smoking all affect safety and healing, so they are discussed and, where possible, improved before a date is set. You will hear openly what a facelift can and cannot do for you.

His technique focuses on lifting the deeper layer so the skin can rest over it without tension. This keeps expressions natural and helps avoid the windswept appearance people worry about. Incisions run in the natural creases in front of and behind the ear, with care taken to keep your hairline and sideburns where they belong.

Like any operation, a facelift has risks, and Dr. Junaid talks you through them at consultation, along with the steps he and his team take to reduce them.

## Techniques and options

Options Dr. Junaid may recommend, depending on your face and goals, include:

- **Mini facelift:** A shorter-scar lift for early jowling and mild looseness in the lower face, with a quicker recovery.
- **SMAS facelift:** Tightens and repositions the firm layer beneath the skin for a more thorough, durable lift of the cheeks and jawline.
- **Deep plane facelift:** Releases and lifts the deeper tissues together, suited to more marked sagging of the mid-face and jowls.
- **Neck lift:** Often added to a facelift to tighten loose neck skin and soften the bands under the chin.
- **Fat transfer:** A small amount of your own fat can restore lost volume in the cheeks alongside the lift.
- **MACS (short-scar) lift:** A version of the mini facelift that lifts the lower face with stitches through a short incision in front of the ear.
- **Endoscopic mid-face lift:** Small hidden incisions and a camera are used to raise the cheek pad and soften the under-eye and nose-to-mouth area.

## What to expect on the day

You come in fasting, meet Dr. Junaid for final markings and see the anaesthetist before going to theatre. Surgery takes around three to five hours under general anaesthesia. You then rest overnight so your dressing, drains and comfort can be checked, and a family member can take you home the next day.

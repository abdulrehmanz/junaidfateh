## About Fat Grafting & Transfer

With age, the face loses volume as well as firmness. Cheeks flatten, temples hollow and shadows form under the eyes, which can make you look tired even when you feel well. Fat grafting, also called fat transfer or lipofilling, replaces some of that lost volume using fat from your own body.

A small amount of fat is gently removed from an area where you have some to spare. It is then cleaned and prepared before being injected in tiny amounts wherever fullness is needed. Because the material is your own, it blends with the surrounding tissue and feels soft to the touch.

This page focuses on facial fat grafting with Dr. Junaid Fateh, a plastic, cosmetic and reconstructive surgeon in Lahore. Fat transfer to the breasts or buttocks is described on separate procedure pages.

## How Dr. Junaid approaches fat grafting

At your consultation Dr. Junaid studies your face from the front and the side, looking at where volume has been lost and where it has not. Adding fat in the wrong place can make a face look heavy, so the plan is about restoring natural contours rather than simply making things fuller. He also checks possible donor sites and asks about your weight history, since a stable weight helps the result last.

During the procedure the fat is collected with a thin cannula, processed to separate it from fluid and oil, and placed in small droplets across several layers. This careful layering gives the fat the best chance of picking up a blood supply and helps avoid lumps.

Not all of the transferred fat survives, and the amount varies. Dr. Junaid explains this honestly before you decide, and in some cases a second, smaller session is advised. Like any procedure, fat grafting carries risks, including bruising, swelling, lumpiness, uneven results, infection and rare but serious complications. He goes through these with you at consultation so you can make an informed choice.

## Techniques and options

- **Cheek and mid-face restoration:** Rebuilds flattened cheeks for a fresher, gently lifted look without changing your features.
- **Under-eye hollows:** Very fine fat is placed carefully to soften the groove between the lower eyelid and the cheek.
- **Temple filling:** Softens the hollow at the sides of the forehead that can make the face look narrow or drawn.
- **Smile lines and lips:** Small amounts can soften deep lines running from nose to mouth or add subtle volume to thin lips.
- **Scars and contour dents:** Fat can lift some depressed scars and smooth irregularities left by injury or earlier surgery.
- **Combined procedures:** Fat grafting can be paired with a facelift, neck lift or eyelid surgery to restore volume as well as lift.

## What to expect on the day

Most facial fat grafting is a day-case procedure lasting one to two hours under local anesthesia with sedation. Dr. Junaid marks the areas to be treated, collects and prepares the fat, then shapes your face one area at a time. You go home the same day with a small dressing on the donor site, advice on keeping your head raised and a follow-up appointment.

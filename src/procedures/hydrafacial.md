## About HydraFacial

A HydraFacial is a clinical facial that works through several steps in a single session. Your skin is cleansed and gently exfoliated, a mild acid solution loosens dead cells and oil, and a suction tip draws blackheads and debris out of the pores. The final step infuses hydrating serums into the freshly cleansed skin.

Because each step is controlled, the treatment is thorough without being harsh. There is no squeezing of spots and no recovery period, which is why it is popular before weddings, Eid and family gatherings, as well as a regular way to keep skin in good condition. Dr. Junaid Fateh offers HydraFacial treatments at his Lahore clinic as part of his non-surgical aesthetics service.

It helps to keep expectations realistic. A HydraFacial gives skin a clean, hydrated glow and helps with congestion and dullness, but it will not remove deep scars or stubborn pigmentation on its own. For those concerns, it usually forms one part of a broader plan.

## How Dr. Junaid approaches HydraFacial

Even a gentle facial benefits from a medical eye. Before treatment, your skin is assessed for type, sensitivity, active breakouts and pigmentation. This lets the clinical team choose the right peel strength and serums, and spot anything that would be better handled with a different treatment.

Climate plays a part too. Lahore's hot summers, dusty air and long hours in air conditioning can leave skin oily in some places and dehydrated in others, while winter brings dryness of its own. The treatment is adjusted to what your skin needs at the time, and you leave with simple advice on cleansing, moisturising and sun protection so the benefit lasts longer.

Dr. Junaid also uses the assessment to think beyond the facial itself. If your concern is really melasma, acne scarring or early lines, he will explain which treatments are more likely to help and how a HydraFacial might fit alongside them.

## Techniques and options

- **Cleanse and exfoliate:** Removes the surface layer of dead cells to reveal fresher skin.
- **Gentle acid peel:** A mild solution, often containing glycolic and salicylic acids, loosens oil and debris in the pores.
- **Suction extraction:** Clears blackheads and congestion without manual squeezing.
- **Hydrating infusion:** Serums with ingredients such as hyaluronic acid, antioxidants and peptides replenish moisture.
- **Targeted boosters:** Additional serums may be chosen for dullness, uneven tone or breakout-prone skin.

## What to expect on the day

The treatment takes 30 to 45 minutes and you lie back comfortably throughout. There are no needles and no numbing is needed. You can go straight back to your day, ideally with a good sunscreen on.

Many people book a session before a family function or after a long flight home, when skin often feels dry and tired. If you are planning other skin treatments as well, the team will advise on spacing them so your skin is not overloaded.

## About Laser Hair Removal

Laser hair removal uses pulses of concentrated light that are absorbed by the dark pigment in hair. The light turns into heat inside the follicle, damaging the parts that produce new hair while leaving the surrounding skin intact. Over a course of sessions, this reduces how much hair grows back, and the hair that does return is usually finer and slower-growing.

For many people in Pakistan the appeal is simple: less time spent threading, waxing or shaving, and fewer ingrown hairs and dark marks from repeated hair removal. Dr. Junaid Fateh offers laser hair removal at his clinics in Lahore and Layyah for both women and men, on the face and body.

Hair grows in phases, and laser only affects the hairs that are actively growing at the time of treatment. That is why one session is never enough and treatment is spread over several months. Because the laser relies on the contrast between dark hair and the surrounding skin, the colour of your hair matters as much as your skin tone.

## How Dr. Junaid approaches laser hair removal

Brown skin needs particular care. Because the skin itself contains more pigment, laser energy that is too strong or poorly matched can cause burns or patches of darker or lighter skin. Before your first session, Dr. Junaid's team assesses your skin type, hair colour and thickness, and the areas you want treated. A test patch may be carried out so your skin's response can be checked before a full session.

Dr. Junaid also looks for underlying causes. Excess facial or body hair is sometimes linked to hormonal conditions such as PCOS. Laser can still help, but you may need more sessions and occasional maintenance, and it can be worth checking your hormones with your doctor at the same time. He is honest that the goal is long-term reduction, not a promise that every hair will disappear.

Settings are reviewed at each visit as your hair becomes finer and your skin's response becomes clearer, which helps keep treatment both effective and safe over the whole course.

## Techniques and options

- **Face:** Upper lip, chin, jawline, sideburns and cheeks, areas often affected by hormonal hair growth.
- **Underarms:** One of the most requested areas, especially through the hot, humid months.
- **Arms and legs:** Full or partial treatment, planned around your hair pattern.
- **Bikini area:** Treated discreetly in a private room, to the extent you prefer.
- **Back, chest and shoulders:** Larger areas commonly treated in men.
- **Beard line tidying:** Laser can neaten the neckline and cheek line for men prone to razor bumps.

## What to expect on the day

Arrive with the area clean-shaven and free of creams. Protective eyewear is worn throughout, and the laser is passed over the skin in short pulses. Small areas take around 15 minutes and larger areas up to an hour. You can return to your normal day straight afterwards. If you have a tan from summer travel or time outdoors, the session may be postponed until it fades, because tanned skin is more likely to react.

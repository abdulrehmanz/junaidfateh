## About lip lift surgery

With age, the upper lip tends to lengthen and thin. The distance between the base of the nose and the lip grows, the pink part of the lip rolls inward and the upper teeth become hidden. Some people also have a naturally long upper lip from a young age. Either way, the lower face can look older or less expressive than you would like.

A lip lift shortens the stretch of skin that runs from the base of the nose down to the lip. By removing a small, carefully measured strip of skin just beneath the nose, the lip is lifted and turned slightly outward, revealing more of its natural colour and shape. Dr. Junaid Fateh performs lip lift surgery in Lahore as a lasting option for people who want better shape and proportion rather than extra volume.

Many people try filler first. Filler adds fullness but cannot shorten a long lip, and too much of it can make the lip look heavy. A lip lift works on the length and position of the lip instead, which is why the two are sometimes used together.

## How Dr. Junaid approaches a lip lift

At consultation, Dr. Junaid looks at your lip at rest, while you speak and when you smile. He measures the length of your upper lip, how much tooth shows, the shape of your nostrils and nose base, and the balance between your upper and lower lips. These details decide whether a lip lift will help and how much skin can safely be removed.

His priority is a natural, balanced result. Removing too much can leave the lip looking stiff or pulled up, or show too much gum, so he plans conservatively and follows the natural curve of the nose base to keep the scar as hidden as possible.

He is also open about scarring. The scar is usually well hidden, but how any scar heals depends on your skin, and darker skin can be more prone to thicker scars. Like any surgery, a lip lift has risks, including infection, visible or widened scarring, asymmetry, temporary stiffness and changes in sensation. You will have the chance to talk each one through with Dr. Junaid before deciding.

## Techniques and options

The lip lift techniques Dr. Junaid may recommend include:

- **Subnasal (bullhorn) lip lift:** The most widely used technique. A strip of skin shaped like a pair of horns is removed along the base of the nose to shorten the whole upper lip.
- **Italian lip lift:** Two smaller areas of skin are removed beneath each nostril, leaving the centre of the nose base untouched. It suits some nose shapes better.
- **Corner lip lift:** Small amounts of skin are removed at the corners of the mouth to lift corners that turn downward.
- **Combined lip treatment:** A lip lift can be paired with a conservative amount of filler for extra volume, or with other facial procedures when appropriate.

## What to expect on the day

A lip lift is a short procedure, usually 45 to 60 minutes, carried out under local anaesthetic, so you are awake but comfortable throughout. You leave soon afterwards with fine stitches under your nose, advice on cold packs and soft foods, and a date for stitch removal.

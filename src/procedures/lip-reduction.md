## About Lip Reduction Surgery

Full lips are often admired, but when the lips are much larger than the rest of the face they can pull attention away from your eyes and smile. Some people are simply born with very prominent lips. Others have a lower lip that rolls outward, or lips that became uneven after an injury or earlier treatment.

Lip reduction surgery, sometimes called reduction cheiloplasty, removes a carefully measured strip of tissue from the inner part of the lip. The lip is then closed so that it sits further in and looks less bulky. Because the incision lies inside the mouth, the scar is usually not visible when your lips are at rest.

Dr. Junaid Fateh, a plastic, cosmetic and reconstructive surgeon in Lahore, performs lip reduction for both cosmetic and functional concerns, including lips that make it difficult to close the mouth comfortably.

## How Dr. Junaid approaches lip reduction

The consultation starts with your goals. Some people want a subtle change, while others want a clearly smaller lip. Dr. Junaid looks at how your lips relate to your nose, chin and teeth, how much of the inner lip shows when you smile, and whether the two sides match. He also checks that the fullness is not caused by swelling, an allergy or another medical problem.

Planning is deliberately conservative. Taking away too much tissue can leave lips looking thin or tight, and that is very hard to undo, so he aims for a reduction that keeps the lips soft, rounded and expressive. You will have time to talk through the shape you would like and what is realistically achievable for your face.

Safety is part of every step. You will be asked about medicines, smoking and any history of cold sores, because these can affect healing. Like any surgery, lip reduction carries risks such as swelling, infection, numbness and asymmetry, and Dr. Junaid talks you through each of them at consultation so you know what to expect.

## Techniques and options

- **Upper lip reduction:** A strip of tissue is removed from the inside of the upper lip to reduce its height and pout.
- **Lower lip reduction:** A heavy lower lip, or one that turns outward, is reduced so it sits closer to the teeth.
- **Both lips together:** The upper and lower lips are treated in one session for an even, balanced result.
- **Asymmetry correction:** Different amounts are removed from each side to even out lips that do not match.
- **Combined facial procedures:** Where it helps overall balance, lip reduction can sometimes be planned alongside other facial surgery such as chin reshaping.

## What to expect on the day

Lip reduction is a day-case procedure, usually done under local anesthesia and lasting one to two hours. Dr. Junaid marks the planned lines before numbing your lips, so you can see and agree on the plan. Once the procedure is finished you rest for a short while, receive your aftercare instructions and go home the same day, ideally with someone to accompany you.

For the first few evenings, a cold compress, soft food and an extra pillow at night make a real difference to comfort. Keep your follow-up appointment so Dr. Junaid can check healing and answer any questions.

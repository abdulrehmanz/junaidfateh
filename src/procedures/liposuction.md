## About liposuction

Liposuction removes pockets of fat that refuse to shift, however carefully you eat or however often you exercise. Through a few small incisions, a slim tube called a cannula is used to loosen and draw out fat from beneath the skin, reshaping the area so it sits in better proportion with the rest of your body.

It is one of the core specialties of Dr. Junaid Fateh, a plastic, cosmetic and reconstructive surgeon who sees patients in Lahore and Layyah. Common areas include the tummy, flanks, back, thighs, knees, upper arms, and the chin and neck. In men, liposuction is often used on the chest as part of gynecomastia treatment, as well as on the waist and flanks.

It also helps to know what liposuction is not. It is not a weight-loss treatment, it does not tighten loose skin on its own, and it does not remove cellulite. It suits people near a healthy weight who want to refine specific areas.

## How Dr. Junaid approaches liposuction

Every plan begins with a detailed consultation. Dr. Junaid asks about your goals, your medical history, any medicines you take and how your weight has changed over recent years. He then examines each area, checking the thickness of the fat, the quality of your skin and your overall build. This tells him whether liposuction alone will give you the change you are hoping for, or whether another option would suit you better.

His aim is a natural-looking contour rather than an over-done one. Taking out too much fat, or taking it unevenly, can leave dents, ridges or a hollow look. Instead, he blends each treated area into its surroundings so that the waist, hips and thighs flow into one another. Before surgery he marks you while you stand, because fat sits differently when you lie down.

Safety guides every decision. You will have routine blood tests and, where needed, a review by a physician or anaesthetist. Dr. Junaid keeps the volume removed within safe limits for each session and may recommend staging larger plans. Like any surgery, liposuction carries risks, including bruising, swelling, numbness, uneven contours, fluid collection, infection and, rarely, blood clots. Dr. Junaid goes through the risks with you at consultation so you can decide with full information.

If you are preparing for a wedding or Eid, plan around three months ahead, as swelling takes time to settle fully.

## Techniques and options

Options Dr. Junaid may recommend, depending on your body and goals, include:

- **Tumescent liposuction:** The area is first filled with a fluid containing local anaesthetic and a medicine that narrows blood vessels, which reduces bleeding and bruising. It forms the basis of most liposuction today.
- **Suction-assisted liposuction:** A fine cannula is moved through the fat by hand, giving close control over the final shape. It remains reliable for most areas of the body.
- **Power-assisted liposuction:** A gently vibrating cannula can make it easier to treat firmer, more fibrous fat, such as on the back or the male chest.
- **Liposuction with fat transfer:** Fat removed from one area can be purified and placed where volume is wanted, such as the buttocks, breasts or face.
- **Liposuction with skin surgery:** When skin is loose, liposuction can be paired with a tummy tuck, arm lift or thigh lift for a smoother overall result.
- **Small-area liposuction:** The chin, neck, knees or arms can often be treated under local anaesthesia with sedation.
- **VASER liposuction:** Ultrasound energy is used to loosen fat before it is removed. It can be helpful for fibrous areas and for detailed contouring and definition, and Dr. Junaid will explain whether it offers an advantage in your case.

## What to expect on the day

You arrive fasting, and Dr. Junaid reviews the plan with you and marks the areas while you stand. Surgery usually takes one to four hours, depending on how many areas are involved. You wake up wearing a compression garment, and the clinical team helps you take a short walk. Most people go home the same day with a family member or friend.

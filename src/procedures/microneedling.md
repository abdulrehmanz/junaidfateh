## About Microneedling

Microneedling, also called collagen induction therapy, uses a sterile device fitted with very fine needles to make tiny, controlled channels in the skin. Your skin treats these as minor injuries and responds by repairing itself, laying down fresh collagen and elastin as it heals. Over a course of treatments, this can make scars shallower, pores look smaller and skin feel firmer and smoother.

It is one of the more versatile skin treatments available. Dr. Junaid Fateh uses it at his Lahore clinic for acne scarring, enlarged pores, rough texture, early fine lines and, in some cases, stretch marks. It can also be combined with PRP prepared from your own blood. Unlike a scrub or surface treatment, it works within the skin, which is why it can help with scars that creams cannot reach.

Microneedling works gradually. You may see a fresher look within a week or two, but the deeper changes in scars and firmness take months to show, because new collagen forms slowly.

## How Dr. Junaid approaches microneedling

A consultation comes first. Dr. Junaid examines your skin closely, identifies the type of scarring or texture problem, and asks about your skin history, including any tendency to develop dark marks after spots or injuries. This matters in South Asian skin, where too much inflammation can leave pigmentation behind.

He then sets the needle depth for each zone of your face: deeper over thicker, scarred areas and shallower over delicate skin. If your skin needs preparing first, for example to calm active acne, he plans that before starting. He is open about how many sessions you are likely to need and how much improvement is realistic, because deep scars can soften but rarely disappear completely.

Before each session he reviews how your skin responded to the last one, adjusting the depth or timing if there was more redness or darkening than expected. Progress photos taken in the same light help you both see changes that are easy to miss from day to day.

## Techniques and options

- **Microneedling for acne scars:** A series of treatments aimed at rolling and shallow scars, sometimes alongside other scar treatments.
- **Skin rejuvenation:** Lighter treatment for texture, pores and early fine lines.
- **Microneedling with PRP:** Plasma prepared from your own blood is applied during treatment to support healing and collagen building.
- **Stretch marks:** Treatment to improve the texture of stretch marks on the body over several sessions.
- **Combination plans:** Microneedling can be alternated with peels or other treatments as part of a wider skin plan.

## What to expect on the day

Your skin is cleansed and numbing cream is applied. Once it has taken effect, the treatment itself takes around 20 to 30 minutes, within an appointment of 30 to 60 minutes. Your skin will look pink when you leave, and the team will give you clear aftercare instructions to follow at home. Many people book their session just before a weekend or day off, so the redness has eased by the time they are back at work.

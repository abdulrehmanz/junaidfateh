## About mommy makeover

Pregnancy and breastfeeding change a woman's body in ways that are completely normal, yet some of these changes do not reverse with time, diet or exercise. The tummy may be left with loose skin and separated muscles, and the breasts may lose volume or sit lower than before.

A mommy makeover is a combination of procedures, planned together, to address these changes. It usually brings together a tummy tuck and a breast procedure, sometimes with liposuction as well. Dr. Junaid Fateh plans every mommy makeover individually, based on your body and what you would like to change.

This is a personal decision, and there is no right or wrong reason to consider it. Consultations are private and unhurried, and you are welcome to bring someone with you for support.

## How Dr. Junaid approaches mommy makeover

At your consultation, Dr. Junaid asks about your pregnancies, deliveries, breastfeeding and weight, and whether you plan more children. He examines your tummy for loose skin and muscle separation and assesses the size, shape and position of your breasts. A previous caesarean scar can usually be taken away within the new, lower tummy tuck scar.

He then helps you prioritise. Some women want a full combination, while others prefer to focus on one area first. His aim is a natural, balanced result that suits your frame, not an exaggerated change.

Safety shapes the plan. Combining procedures means one anaesthetic and one recovery, but it also makes the operation longer, so Dr. Junaid keeps total surgical time within safe limits and may stage your plan if needed. Like any surgery, a mommy makeover carries risks, including bleeding, infection, fluid collection, wound healing problems, changes in nipple sensation and blood clots. Dr. Junaid goes through the risks with you at consultation.

Practical planning matters too. Most women need help at home with children and housework for the first few weeks, so it helps to involve your family early.

## Techniques and options

Every plan is different. Options Dr. Junaid may recommend, depending on your concerns, include:

- **Tummy tuck with muscle repair:** Removes loose lower tummy skin and stitches separated muscles back together for a firmer core.
- **Breast lift:** Raises and reshapes breasts that have dropped after breastfeeding, with or without added volume.
- **Breast augmentation:** Restores lost fullness with implants or, in suitable cases, with your own fat.
- **Breast reduction:** Reduces breasts that have stayed heavier after pregnancy, easing neck and back discomfort.
- **Liposuction:** Refines the waist, flanks, back or thighs for a smoother outline.

## What to expect on the day

Surgery is done under general anaesthesia and usually takes four to six hours, depending on the combination. A stay of one or two nights lets the clinical team keep you comfortable and help you up for your first short walks.

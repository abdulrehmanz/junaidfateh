## About Neck Lift (Cervicoplasty)

The neck is often one of the first areas to show age, and one of the hardest to improve with creams or exercise. Over time the skin loses its spring, fat gathers under the chin and the thin platysma muscle at the front of the neck can separate into visible cords. The jawline softens and many people start to notice the loose look often called a "turkey neck".

A neck lift, also known as cervicoplasty, deals with these changes directly. Depending on what is causing your concern, it can lift and trim excess skin, reduce fat beneath the chin and tighten the muscle underneath. The aim is a cleaner angle between jaw and neck that looks like a fresher version of you, not a different person.

Dr. Junaid Fateh is a plastic, cosmetic and reconstructive surgeon practising in Lahore. He performs neck lifts as a standalone operation or together with a facelift when the lower face has also descended.

## How Dr. Junaid approaches neck lift surgery

Every plan begins with a careful examination. Dr. Junaid assesses your skin quality, how much fat sits above and below the muscle, how strong any neck bands are and how your chin and jaw are shaped. He also asks about your health, medicines and smoking, because these affect healing. If you smoke, you will be asked to stop for a period before and after surgery, since nicotine reduces blood flow to the skin.

He then explains which parts of your neck need attention and which can be left alone. Some people need little more than fat reduction and muscle tightening through a short cut under the chin. Others need skin removal through incisions that run around the ears. You will see exactly where the scars will sit and how they are tucked into natural creases and the hairline.

A natural result matters more than a dramatic one. Over-tightening can leave the neck looking pulled or flat, so Dr. Junaid aims for a smooth, gently defined contour that suits your age and face. Safety runs through every step: he reviews your medical history, may arrange routine tests before surgery, and goes through the risks with you so you can make an informed decision.

## Techniques and options

- **Neck liposuction:** Removes stubborn fat under the chin and along the jaw through tiny openings. It works best when the skin is still firm enough to shrink back on its own.
- **Platysmaplasty:** Draws together and tightens the edges of the platysma muscle, usually through a short incision under the chin, to soften vertical bands.
- **Skin-tightening neck lift:** Lifts and redrapes loose skin, removing the excess through incisions hidden around and behind the ears.
- **Combined face and neck lift:** Treats jowls and neck in one operation when both have dropped, so the whole lower face looks balanced.
- **Non-surgical alternatives:** For mild laxity or a small double chin, options Dr. Junaid may recommend include non-surgical skin-tightening or fat-reducing treatments. He will explain how their results compare with surgery.

## What to expect on the day

You arrive having fasted as instructed. Dr. Junaid reviews the plan with you, marks your neck and then the anesthesia is given. Surgery usually takes two to three hours. Afterwards a supportive chin strap or dressing is put on, and most people go home the same day or after one night with written aftercare instructions and a follow-up appointment.

## About Nipple & Areola Correction

Correcting the nipple or areola involves small, precise operations that change the shape, size or projection of the nipple and the darker skin around it. Common concerns include nipples that are flat or turned inwards, areolae that have become large or stretched, nipples that are long or prominent, and differences between the two sides.

These concerns affect women and men alike. Some appear at puberty, while others develop after pregnancy, breastfeeding or weight change. Although the operations are short, they involve a very private part of the body, and Dr. Junaid Fateh treats every consultation with discretion and sensitivity.

Most corrections are done under local anaesthetic in less than an hour, and you go home the same day.

## How Dr. Junaid approaches nipple and areola correction

Dr. Junaid starts by understanding exactly what bothers you and examining the area closely. If a nipple has recently turned inward, has discharge or has changed in appearance, he will make sure this is checked before any cosmetic treatment, because such changes can occasionally point to an underlying breast problem.

He will also ask whether you may want to breastfeed in future, as this affects the choice of technique for inverted nipples. The plan is based on the proportions of your breast or chest, so the nipple and areola look natural rather than artificially perfect.

Risks are generally low but include infection, altered nipple sensation, a scar that stretches or stays visible, slight asymmetry and, with inverted nipples, the chance that the nipple turns inward again. Dr. Junaid covers each of these at your consultation.

## Techniques and options

Depending on what needs correcting, options Dr. Junaid may recommend include:

- **Inverted nipple correction:** the tight bands pulling the nipple inward are released through a tiny incision, and internal stitches help it stay out. Milk ducts are preserved where the degree of inversion allows.
- **Areola reduction:** a ring of pigmented skin is removed from the outer edge and the border is closed with a fine supporting stitch, so the scar lies along the natural colour change.
- **Nipple reduction:** the height or width of a long or wide nipple is reduced while keeping its blood supply.
- **Puffy nipple correction for men:** a small amount of gland and fat behind the areola is removed, either on its own or as part of gynecomastia surgery.
- **Nipple reconstruction:** a new nipple is shaped from local skin after breast reconstruction, with colour added later by medical tattooing.

## What to expect on the day

You stay awake while the area is numbed, and fasting is not usually needed unless sedation is planned. The procedure takes about 30 to 60 minutes, after which a light dressing is applied and you can go home with a companion.

## About Non-Surgical Rhinoplasty

Non-surgical rhinoplasty, sometimes called a liquid rhinoplasty, uses small amounts of hyaluronic acid filler to change how the nose looks in profile and from the front. Filler placed above and below a bump can make the bridge appear straighter. Support at the base of the tip can make it look slightly lifted, and filling a dip or uneven area can improve overall balance.

Dr. Junaid Fateh offers non-surgical rhinoplasty at his clinic in Lahore. As a plastic surgeon who also performs surgical rhinoplasty, he can help you decide which route suits you. For many people, filler is a useful way to refine a nose they mostly like, or to preview a change before committing to surgery.

It is important to understand the limits. Filler adds volume, so the nose cannot be made smaller, narrower or shorter, and breathing problems are not improved. The change is also temporary, lasting many months rather than for life. If you are hoping to look your best for a wedding or another big occasion, it is wise to have treatment several weeks ahead so any swelling has fully settled.

## How Dr. Junaid approaches non-surgical rhinoplasty

The nose is one of the more delicate areas to treat with filler, because its blood vessels lie close to the surface and connect with vessels around the eyes. Dr. Junaid's training as a plastic surgeon gives him a detailed understanding of this anatomy, and he plans every injection with safety first.

At consultation, he looks at your nose in relation to your forehead, lips and chin, since a balanced profile depends on all of them. He explains what filler can realistically achieve for your nose and whether surgery would serve you better. During treatment he uses small amounts, injects slowly and watches the skin closely as he goes. You leave knowing the warning signs to look out for and how to reach the clinical team quickly.

If you have had filler in your nose before, bring any details you have, since previous product can affect what is safe to add.

## Techniques and options

- **Dorsal hump camouflage:** Filler placed above and below a bump to make the bridge appear straighter.
- **Tip support:** A small amount at the base of the tip to give a subtle lift.
- **Bridge definition:** Building up a low or flat bridge for a more defined profile.
- **Asymmetry correction:** Evening out minor dents or a slight deviation.
- **Post-surgical refinement:** Smoothing small irregularities after previous rhinoplasty, once healing is complete and after careful assessment.

## What to expect on the day

Photos are taken and numbing cream is applied. The treatment itself usually takes 15 to 30 minutes, and you will see the result in the mirror straight away. Mild swelling settles quickly, and you can return to your normal routine with a few simple precautions. A review a couple of weeks later lets Dr. Junaid see the settled result and make any small refinement.

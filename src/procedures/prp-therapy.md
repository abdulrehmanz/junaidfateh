## About PRP Therapy

Platelets are the cells in your blood that help wounds heal. They carry growth factors, natural signalling proteins that encourage tissue repair and the production of new collagen. PRP, short for platelet-rich plasma, concentrates these platelets from a small sample of your own blood so they can be returned to the skin where they are needed.

On the face, PRP is used to improve skin quality: a dull complexion, early fine lines, crepey skin under the eyes and mild acne scarring. When it is combined with microneedling, the treatment is often called a vampire facial. Dr. Junaid Fateh offers PRP as part of his non-surgical aesthetics work in Lahore.

It is worth being clear about what to expect. PRP is a gentle, gradual treatment rather than a dramatic one. Changes tend to be subtle, appear over weeks and build across a course of sessions. Research into PRP for the skin is still developing, and results vary between people.

## How Dr. Junaid approaches PRP therapy

Dr. Junaid starts by examining your skin and asking what you would most like to change. If PRP alone is unlikely to meet your goals, for example with deep lines or marked volume loss, he will say so and explain the alternatives, such as fillers, peels or a combined plan.

Preparation matters. Blood is drawn using sterile technique, spun in a centrifuge so the plasma separates out, and used fresh during the same visit. Dr. Junaid then decides whether to inject it, apply it with microneedling, or both, depending on your concern. He also checks your medicines and health history, since some conditions make PRP less suitable or less effective.

Because PRP works best as a course, he also sets out a realistic timeline at the start, with review points along the way. Photos taken before your first session make it easier to judge progress fairly, rather than relying on memory.

## Techniques and options

- **PRP with microneedling (vampire facial):** Fine needles create tiny channels while PRP is applied over the skin, combining collagen stimulation with growth factors.
- **Injected PRP:** Small amounts are placed under the skin with a fine needle to treat specific areas.
- **Under-eye PRP:** A gentle option for thin, crepey skin under the eyes; it does not replace filler where hollows are deep.
- **PRP for acne scars:** Usually combined with microneedling or other scar treatments over several sessions.
- **PRP as an add-on:** Used alongside other skin treatments as part of a wider plan when it is likely to help.
- **PRP for wound and soft-tissue healing:** Used in selected slow-healing wounds or after some procedures to support the body's own repair, as part of a wider treatment plan.

## What to expect on the day

A small amount of blood is taken from your arm, much like a routine blood test. While it is being prepared, numbing cream is applied to your face. The whole appointment takes 30 to 60 minutes, and you go home the same day with clear aftercare advice. Your face may look pink and slightly puffy for the rest of the day, so many people book in the afternoon or before a day off.

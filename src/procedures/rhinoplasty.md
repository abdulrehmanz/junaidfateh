## About rhinoplasty

Your nose sits at the centre of your face, so even a small change to its shape can alter how your eyes, cheeks, lips and chin look together. Rhinoplasty, often called a nose job, reshapes the bone and cartilage of the nose to bring it into better balance with the rest of your features. Dr. Junaid Fateh, a plastic, cosmetic and reconstructive surgeon in Lahore, performs rhinoplasty to improve both appearance and function.

People come to him with many different concerns: a bump on the bridge, a tip that looks heavy or droops when they smile, nostrils that feel too wide, or a nose that has been crooked since a cricket ball or a fall. Others mainly want to breathe properly, especially at night or during exercise. In many cases both issues can be treated in a single operation.

Rhinoplasty is one of the most detailed operations in facial surgery. A change of a millimetre or two makes a visible difference, and the nose keeps refining as swelling settles over many months. That is why careful planning and realistic expectations matter as much as the surgery itself.

## How Dr. Junaid approaches rhinoplasty

Your consultation begins with listening. Dr. Junaid asks what bothers you, how you see your nose in photos and in the mirror, and whether you have any breathing complaints. He then examines the outside and inside of your nose, looking at skin thickness, the strength of the cartilage, the septum and the airway.

He will talk you through what is realistic for your anatomy. Thick skin, for example, limits how sharp a tip can become, while a small chin can make a nose look larger than it really is. Where it helps, he looks at your profile as a whole. Photographs are taken to support planning and to track your healing.

His aim is a natural-looking nose that keeps your identity. He prefers measured changes that respect your ethnic features over a dramatic look that can seem out of place on your face. Breathing is protected throughout, because a nose that looks good but blocks easily is not a good result.

Safety runs through every step, from a review of your medical history and medicines to routine tests before surgery. Like any surgery, rhinoplasty carries some risk, which Dr. Junaid discusses openly at consultation so you can decide with confidence.

## Techniques and options

Options Dr. Junaid may recommend include:

- **Closed rhinoplasty:** All incisions are hidden inside the nostrils. It suits moderate changes such as lowering a hump or narrowing the bridge.
- **Open rhinoplasty:** A small cut across the columella, the skin between the nostrils, gives a direct view of the tip structures. It is useful for tip refinement and more complex reshaping.
- **Septorhinoplasty:** A deviated septum is straightened alongside the cosmetic changes, improving airflow as well as appearance.
- **Revision rhinoplasty:** Corrects problems left after earlier nose surgery. This is often more involved, and cartilage grafts may be needed to rebuild support.
- **Non-surgical rhinoplasty:** Carefully placed dermal filler can smooth a small bump or lift the tip for a temporary change without an operation. It cannot make the nose smaller or fix breathing.
- **Ultrasonic (piezo) rhinoplasty:** Bone is reshaped with a piezoelectric instrument that vibrates to sculpt bone while sparing the surrounding soft tissue. It allows precise refinement of the nasal bones and can mean less bruising and swelling for some patients.

## What to expect on the day

You arrive having fasted as instructed. After a final check with Dr. Junaid and the anaesthetic team, the operation usually takes two to four hours. You wake with a small splint on your nose and either leave the same day with a relative or stay one night. Swelling and bruising tend to build over the first two to three days, then steadily improve.

## About thigh lift

A thigh lift, sometimes called thighplasty, removes loose skin and some fat from the thighs to give a smoother, firmer shape. Most often it treats the inner thighs, where skin tends to sag and rub after major weight loss or as it loses elasticity with age.

For many people the main problem is comfort rather than appearance. Loose inner thigh skin can chafe painfully when you walk, especially in the heat and humidity of a Pakistani summer, leading to rashes and broken skin. A thigh lift can ease this as well as improving how your legs look and how clothes fit.

Dr. Junaid Fateh performs thigh lifts in Lahore, either as a single procedure or as one stage of a wider plan after weight loss.

## How Dr. Junaid approaches thigh lift

At consultation, Dr. Junaid examines where the loose skin sits, how far it extends down the leg and how much fat is present. This decides whether a short groin-crease scar will be enough or whether a longer vertical scar is needed to remove skin running towards the knee.

He plans scars to sit in natural creases and under underwear wherever possible, and aims for a natural, even contour rather than an over-tight one. Liposuction may be added to refine the shape.

If you are planning other body contouring as well, he will advise whether the thigh lift should be done on its own or as one stage of a wider plan, because healing in the groin needs your full attention in the early weeks.

The groin is an area that moves, sweats and bends constantly, so careful wound care matters. Like any surgery, a thigh lift carries risks, including wound separation, infection, fluid collection, swelling in the legs, scars that widen or shift and, rarely, blood clots. Dr. Junaid goes through the risks with you at consultation, along with how to care for the wounds at home.

## Techniques and options

For the thighs, options Dr. Junaid may recommend include:

- **Inner (medial) thigh lift:** A scar in the groin crease lifts and tightens loose skin at the top of the inner thigh.
- **Extended inner thigh lift:** Adds a scar along the inner thigh to remove skin that runs down towards the knee, often after major weight loss.
- **Mini thigh lift:** For mild looseness close to the groin, a shorter scar may be enough.
- **Outer or circumferential thigh lift:** Treats loose skin on the outer thigh or all the way around the leg, usually alongside a lower body lift.
- **Thigh lift with liposuction:** Combines fat reduction with skin removal for a smoother result.

## What to expect on the day

The operation takes two to three hours under general anaesthesia. You wake up in a compression garment and usually stay one night so the clinical team can monitor you and help you take your first steps.

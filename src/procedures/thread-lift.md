## About Thread Lift

As the face ages, the soft tissues of the cheeks and jawline gradually descend. Jowls form, the jawline loses its crisp edge and the midface can look flatter and more tired. A thread lift uses fine, dissolvable threads placed under the skin to reposition some of that tissue and give a gentle lift without a surgical incision.

The threads are made of PDO (polydioxanone), a material long used in dissolvable surgical stitches. Some threads have tiny barbs or cogs that grip the tissue to lift it; others are smooth and used mainly to improve skin quality. Over the following months the threads dissolve, and the body lays down new collagen around them.

Dr. Junaid Fateh performs thread lifts at his clinic in Lahore. As a plastic surgeon who also carries out surgical facelifts, he can give you a balanced view of what threads can achieve and when surgery would give a better result.

## How Dr. Junaid approaches thread lifts

At consultation, Dr. Junaid examines your face while you sit upright, assesses how far the tissues have descended and checks the thickness and elasticity of your skin. He then explains honestly whether threads are likely to meet your goals. Threads suit mild to moderate sagging. They cannot remove excess skin, and they will not match the result of a facelift.

If threads are right for you, he plans the direction and number of threads to lift along a natural line, keeping the result balanced on both sides. He prefers a subtle refresh to an over-tightened look, and may suggest combining threads with filler where volume loss is part of the picture. Sterile technique and careful placement away from important nerves and vessels are part of every treatment.

## Techniques and options

- **Barbed (cog) PDO threads:** Grip the tissue to give a visible lift to the cheeks, jowls and jawline.
- **Smooth PDO threads:** Placed in a fine mesh pattern to support skin quality and firmness over time.
- **Midface lift:** Lifts the cheeks and can soften the folds that run from the nose to the mouth.
- **Jawline and jowl lift:** Helps restore a cleaner jawline when jowling is still at an early stage.
- **Brow lift:** Gives a gentle lift to the outer brow for a more open, rested look.
- **Neck:** Smooth threads can help mild crepey neck skin; more advanced laxity needs a different approach.

## What to expect on the day

You have time to ask questions before treatment begins. The entry points are marked and numbed with local anaesthetic, and the threads are placed using a fine needle or blunt cannula. The procedure takes 30 to 60 minutes and you go home the same day. You may feel tightness and notice small dimples at the entry points, both of which usually settle over the following days. A follow-up visit lets Dr. Junaid check how the threads have settled.

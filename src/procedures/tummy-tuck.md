## About tummy tuck

A tummy tuck, or abdominoplasty, removes loose skin and excess fat from the lower abdomen and, in most cases, tightens the muscles underneath. The result is a flatter, firmer front and a better-defined waist. It is often the most effective choice when the problem is loose skin rather than fat alone, something liposuction cannot correct by itself.

Dr. Junaid Fateh performs tummy tucks in Lahore for women after pregnancy and for men and women who have lost a large amount of weight. Pregnancy can stretch the skin beyond its ability to recover and pull the two long tummy muscles apart, a condition called diastasis recti. This can leave a rounded lower tummy that does not respond to dieting or sit-ups.

A tummy tuck is not a way to lose weight. It works well when you are already close to a stable, healthy weight and want to deal with the skin and muscle changes left behind.

## How Dr. Junaid approaches tummy tuck

At consultation, Dr. Junaid examines how much loose skin you have above and below the navel, checks for muscle separation and looks for any hernia around the belly button. He asks about previous operations, including caesarean sections, as well as smoking, medical conditions and whether you plan more children. These details decide which type of tummy tuck suits you.

Scar placement is planned with you. He marks the line low enough to sit under your underwear and the waistband of your usual clothes, and shapes the navel so it looks natural rather than obviously operated on. Where the waist or flanks also carry extra fat, he may add liposuction for a smoother outline from front to side.

Safety comes first throughout. You will have blood tests and a pre-operative check, and steps are taken to lower the risk of blood clots, including early walking after surgery. Like any surgery, a tummy tuck carries risks, such as fluid collecting under the skin, slow wound healing, infection, numbness, a wider scar than hoped and, rarely, blood clots. Dr. Junaid goes through the risks with you at consultation.

## Techniques and options

Depending on your examination, options Dr. Junaid may recommend include:

- **Full tummy tuck:** Treats the whole abdomen with a low hip-to-hip scar and a small scar around the navel. Muscle repair is usually included.
- **Mini tummy tuck:** Suits a small amount of loose skin below the navel only. The scar is shorter and the navel is left in place.
- **Extended tummy tuck:** The scar continues further around the hips to treat loose skin on the flanks, often after weight loss.
- **Fleur-de-lis tummy tuck:** Adds a vertical scar so skin can be removed from side to side as well as top to bottom, for very loose skin after major weight loss.
- **Tummy tuck with liposuction:** Combines skin removal with fat reduction on the waist and flanks for a more even contour.
- **Muscle repair for diastasis recti:** The separated muscles are stitched together to firm the core and narrow the waist.

## What to expect on the day

Surgery takes two to four hours under general anaesthesia. You wake up with a binder around your tummy and possibly small drains. Most people stay one or two nights so the clinical team can manage pain and help you walk.
